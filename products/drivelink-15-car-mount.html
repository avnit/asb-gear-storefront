Questions about the DriveLink 15

Will it hold my phone in a thick case?

Through a case up to about 3 mm, yes — the N52 array is specified at roughly 1.6 kg of holding force. Cases thicker than that, or cases with metal plates in the back, weaken both the magnetic hold and the charging rate.

Does it work with Android phones?

Yes. Qi2 Android handsets attach and charge magnetically at their rated speed. Phones without built-in magnets use the included adhesive ring and charge at the standard Qi rate their hardware supports (typically 7.5 W).

Why does a car charger need a fan?

Wireless charging is only about 70–80% efficient — the rest becomes heat, on top of an already hot cabin. Passive chargers hit their thermal limit and drop to a few watts. The fan is what keeps the DriveLink at its rated output.

Will the vent clip damage my vents?

The clip is designed for horizontal slats and spreads load across a wide contact pad. For round, delicate, or unusually thin vents, use the included dashboard base instead.

Is a car adapter included?

Yes — a 30 W USB-C 12 V adapter and a 1.5 m braided cable are both in the box. You don't need to buy anything else.
