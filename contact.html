Frequently asked

When can I actually buy?

Both products are in pre-production. Reservations are open now and hold the introductory price; ordering opens once production units clear inspection and payment processing is live. Everyone who reserved is emailed first.

Is my reservation binding?

No, in either direction. No payment is taken, you can cancel any time before you approve the final price, and we're not committed to supply until we send a dispatch confirmation. See the Terms.

Why isn't checkout working?

It's deliberate. We haven't connected a payment processor yet, and we're not going to put card fields on a page that can't charge them. The reservation flow is the real mechanism until then.

Will the introductory price hold?

Yes, for anyone who reserved before launch — $89 for the Nexus 9 and $49 for the DriveLink 15, against planned retail of $109 and $59.

Do you ship outside the US and Canada?

Not at launch. EU and UK follow once conformity assessment is complete. See Shipping.

Can I see real photos rather than illustrations?

Not yet — the graphics on this site are illustrations, and we'd rather label them as such than pass off a render as a photo. Production photography goes up the moment we have units in hand.

Are the published specifications final?

They're the target specification we're building to, and they're still being confirmed against production samples. Anything that changes will be corrected on the product pages before ordering opens, and reservation holders will be told directly if a material spec moves.
