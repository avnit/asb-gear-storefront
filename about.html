About

A hardware line with a very short catalogue.

ASB Gear is the hardware product line of ASB Solutions Group Inc., a US S-corporation whose day job is cloud and AI engineering. We spend a lot of time at desks and in cars with hardware that doesn't quite work, so we built the two pieces we kept wanting.

How we decide what to make

A product joins the catalogue only if we can name the specific compromise everyone else made and say why we didn't make it. That's a slow filter, which is why there are two products and not forty.

For the Nexus 9 that compromise is thermal: most docks in the $60–$100 range put a hot controller in a plastic box and let it throttle. For the DriveLink 15 it's the same problem in a hotter place — a passive magnetic charger in a sunlit car drops to a trickle within fifteen minutes, so ours has a fan.

What we publish

  • Both display numbers. Single-monitor and dual-monitor refresh rates, not just the flattering one.
  • Real charging rates. Certified Qi2 15 W, with the fallback rates for non-Qi2 phones listed beside it.
  • The full box contents. Including the car adapter and cable, because "adapter sold separately" is a price trick.
  • What isn't included. The Nexus 9 does not ship with a power adapter, and that's stated on its product page rather than discovered on arrival.

Warranty and support

Two years from delivery on both products. Support is one email address, answered by people who can authorise a replacement: support@asbsolutionsgroup.com. If a unit fails inside the warranty period we ship a replacement and send a prepaid label for the old one.

The company

ASB Solutions Group Inc. is a US S-corporation working in Google Cloud security, AI integration, and cloud-native engineering. Its consulting practice is at asbsolutionsgroup.com. ASB Gear operates as its hardware line, under the same entity and the same warranty obligations.
