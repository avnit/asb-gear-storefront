What "engineered properly" actually means

Four commitments we'd rather be held to than advertise around.

No borrowed certifications

We don't print a certification mark we haven't earned, and we don't use "compatible with" to imply one. If a number is on this page, we can show you where it came from.

Thermals designed first

Aluminium housings and, in the DriveLink, an actual fan. Heat is the reason cheap chargers slow down after ten minutes.

2-year warranty, no runaround

Two years from delivery. One email to support, a replacement unit, and a prepaid return label for the old one.

Common questions

When can I actually buy these?

Both products are in pre-production. Checkout opens once payment processing is live — everyone on the launch list is emailed before general availability.

Are the prices shown final?

The introductory prices ($89 and $49) are held for the launch list. The struck-through figures are the planned regular retail prices.

Where do you ship?

United States and Canada at launch, with the EU and UK following once compliance marking is complete.

Does the DriveLink work with a non-magnetic phone?

Yes — with the included adhesive metal ring. Android handsets without built-in magnets attach through the ring and charge at the standard wireless-charging rate their hardware supports.

Who is ASB Solutions Group Inc.?

A US S-corporation operating in cloud and AI consulting, at asbsolutionsgroup.com. ASB Gear is its hardware product line.
