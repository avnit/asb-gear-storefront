30-day returns

If a product isn't right for you, send it back within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the purchase price. You don't need to give a reason.

  • The product must be complete — all cables, mounts, adapters, and accessories included.
  • Reasonable inspection is fine. A product that has been used normally and shows light handling marks is still returnable; one that has been damaged, opened, or modified is not.
  • We pay return shipping when the product is faulty, damaged in transit, or not what you ordered. For change-of-mind returns, return shipping is yours.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 10 business days of the returned unit arriving and passing inspection.

Two-year limited warranty

Both the Nexus 9 and the DriveLink 15 are warranted against defects in materials and workmanship for 24 months from the date of delivery, to the original purchaser, for normal use as described in the product documentation.

What we'll do

If a covered defect appears, we replace the unit. We ship the replacement first and include a prepaid label for the faulty one — you're not left without hardware while a claim is assessed. If we can't replace it, we refund the purchase price.

A note on connected devices

Our products are designed and tested to the relevant USB and wireless-power specifications. That said, this warranty covers our product — it does not extend to laptops, phones, monitors, or other equipment connected to it. If you believe one of our products damaged your device, contact us: we will investigate properly rather than point at the exclusion, but any liability is subject to the limits in our Terms.

How to make a claim

  1. Email support@asbsolutionsgroup.com with your order number, the product, and what's happening. A photo or short video helps.
  2. We reply within two business days, usually with an answer rather than a form.
  3. If it's a covered defect, we issue an RMA number and ship the replacement.
  4. You return the faulty unit with the prepaid label. That's it.

We don't require the original packaging, a receipt photo, or a phone call. Your order number is enough.

Your statutory rights

This warranty is in addition to, and does not affect, your rights under applicable consumer law. Some jurisdictions do not allow limits on how long an implied warranty lasts or the exclusion of incidental or consequential damages, so some of the limitations above may not apply to you. This warranty gives you specific legal rights, and you may have other rights which vary by state.
