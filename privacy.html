Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 July 2026

Draft pending legal review. This policy describes how the site works today. It needs review by counsel — and re-writing — before any analytics, payment processor, or email platform is added, because each of those changes what is collected.

The short version

This site is static HTML. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, loads no tracking pixels, and has no advertising integrations. The only personal data we receive is what you deliberately send us by email. There is no database behind this website.

Who controls your data

ASB Solutions Group Inc., [REGISTERED ADDRESS]. For any privacy question or request, write to privacy@asbsolutionsgroup.com.

What we collect, and why

DataWhenWhyKept for
Name, email, phone, shipping address Only if you complete the reservation form and send the email it generates To hold your allocation and contact you at launch Until launch concludes, then 24 months, unless you ask us to erase it sooner
Email address alone If you join the launch list To tell you when ordering opens Until you unsubscribe or ask us to erase it
Anything in a support email When you contact us To answer you, and to handle warranty claims Warranty period plus 12 months
Cart contents As you browse To remember your selections between pages Stored only in your own browser — never transmitted to us

Your browser's local storage

The cart uses your browser's localStorage to remember which items you selected. This is not a cookie, it is never sent to our servers, and it contains only product codes and quantities — no personal information. Clearing your browser data empties the cart. Because nothing is transmitted, no consent banner is required for it.

Who we share it with

Nobody, at present. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. When we begin shipping we will need to pass your address to a carrier and your payment details to a payment processor — this policy will be updated to name them before that happens.

Our email is hosted by our mail provider and this site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which receives standard web-server request logs including IP addresses. We do not control or have access to those logs beyond what GitHub provides.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you what we hold, correct it, delete it, or stop using it. Email privacy@asbsolutionsgroup.com and we will respond within 30 days. We will not charge you or make you justify the request.

If you are a California resident, the CCPA/CPRA gives you rights to know, delete, correct, and opt out of "sale" or "sharing" of personal information — we do neither, and we do not discriminate against anyone who exercises these rights. If you are in the EU, EEA, or UK, note that we are not currently shipping to those regions; if you contact us anyway, we process your message on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering it, and you may complain to your local supervisory authority.

Children

These products are not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Because there is no account system, no database, and no payment processing, there is very little stored to breach. Reservation emails live in our mailbox under normal access controls. When payment processing is introduced, card details will be handled entirely by the processor on their own hosted pages — we will not receive or store card numbers at any point.

Changes

We will update this page as the site gains functionality, and revise the date above. Material changes affecting existing reservations will be emailed to the address you gave us.
