Shipping

Last updated 23 July 2026

Not yet operative. Nothing ships until production units clear inspection. The rates and transit times below are our intended launch structure and are not yet contracted with a carrier — they will be confirmed, and this page updated, before any order is taken.

Where we ship

At launch: the United States (including Alaska and Hawaii) and Canada. The EU and UK follow once CE and UKCA conformity assessment is complete — joining the launch list is the fastest way to hear when that happens.

We do not currently ship to PO boxes for the DriveLink 15, because the included car adapter ships as a separate carrier line item.

Processing time

Orders placed before 2pm ET on a business day are picked the same day; otherwise the next business day. Transit times above start when the carrier scans the parcel, not when you click pay. We don't dispatch on weekends or US public holidays.

Tracking

Every order ships tracked. You'll get the tracking number by email the moment the label is scanned. If tracking hasn't updated for five business days, contact us — we'll open a carrier trace rather than ask you to wait it out.

Customs and duties

Canadian orders may attract duties, GST/HST, or a carrier brokerage fee on delivery. These are set by Canadian authorities and the carrier, not by us, and are the recipient's responsibility. We declare shipments accurately at full commercial value — we will not mark an order as a gift or under-declare it.

Lost, delayed, or damaged parcels

  • Damaged in transit: photograph the parcel and contents, email us within 7 days of delivery, and we send a replacement. We deal with the carrier, not you.
  • Marked delivered but missing: tell us. We'll check the carrier's GPS scan data and, in most cases, simply reship. We're not going to make you file a police report over a $49 charger.
  • Delayed beyond the estimate: the estimates above are estimates, not guarantees. If a parcel is more than 10 business days past its window we'll reship or refund, your choice.

Wrong address

We ship to the address you give us. If it's wrong, email us immediately — before dispatch we can change it for free. After dispatch we can attempt a carrier intercept, but if the parcel is delivered to the address you supplied, a reshipment is at your cost.

Bulk and wholesale

Ordering more than 10 units, or shipping to a business address in volume? Email sales@asbsolutionsgroup.com — freight works out considerably cheaper than 10 individual parcels and we'll quote it properly.
